## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-02, em dash as UI text.** `src/app/(app)/admin/users/page.tsx:81` renders `—` as the "no action" cell. `src/components/ui/table.tsx:7` treats `—`, `–` and `-` as "blank" in a regex (detection logic, keep it matching whatever replacement is chosen). Fix: render an empty cell with a visually hidden "No actions" label, or a plain hyphen, and keep the regex in sync.
2. **R-02, en dash as a separator in headings.** `projects/[code]/page.tsx:151` and `request/[code]/page.tsx:36` build titles as `Company – Project`. `import-wizard.tsx:547` uses `–` for empty values. Fix: comma or colon (`Company, Project`), and a word such as "none" for empty values.
3. **R-02 / R-37, the app mixes English and Indonesian.** The same screen has "Sign in" and "Gunakan akun Halcom Dashboard Anda", "Switch account" and "Akun yang diingat…", "Forget" next to "Belum ada project", "Planned widgets" above an Indonesian explanation. Navigation, buttons and table headers are English; summaries, empty states and placeholders are mostly Indonesian. **Needs a decision:** one UI language, or English for labels and Indonesian for sentences with a written rule. Affects about 40 strings (login, `definitions.ts`, page `summary` props, empty states, placeholders).
4. **R-36, unevidenced claim in the error page.** `src/app/(app)/error.tsx:19` says "Your data is safe." The page cannot know that. Fix: "Reload the page. If it keeps failing, send this reference to an administrator."
5. **R-27, missing UI states.** There is no `not-found.tsx` and no `loading.tsx`; the fallbacks are a bare "Loading…" line (`page-fallback.tsx`, `(app)/layout.tsx:24`, `password-change/page.tsx:24`). Fix: a branded 404 with a link back to the person's own dashboard, and a skeleton-free loading line that names the page ("Loading projects…"). Error and empty states exist and are fine.
6. **R-03, long placeholders (verify at 360px).** `projects/page.tsx:152` ("Cari kode, customer, project, PIC, principal, atau external source"), `request-list.tsx:168` and `crosscheck/page.tsx:61` are long enough to be cut off on a phone. Fix: short placeholder ("Search projects") and move the searchable fields into a hint under the box.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

7. **R-09, status badge on every planned card.** `planned-dashboard.tsx:23` puts "No data yet" on each widget. The state is the same for all four, so it is noise (and it says it in two languages). Fix: one notice above the list, no per-card badge.
8. **R-16 / C-5, dev-voice copy shown to users.** `planned-dashboard.tsx:15` ("Kolom ini tetap kosong daripada menampilkan angka karangan"), `backups/page.tsx:63` ("the host folder is set by BACKUP_HOST_DIR. No retention is applied yet."), `system/page.tsx:38` ("history is not recorded yet"). These explain implementation to the reader. Fix: say what the person can do or expect ("Management widgets appear once SLA targets are set").
9. **R-06, uppercase label style used as the default.** `text-xs tracking-wide uppercase` appears on stat tiles, sidebar group labels and tagline, form legends, definition lists, and the From/To log (about 15 places). Tracking is mild, but the pattern is the default for every small label. Fix: sentence case at 12-13px with medium weight; keep uppercase for none, or write the reason for one place.
10. **R-36, page summaries are long and repeat the title.** Many `DashboardHeader` summaries are 30-60 words in 2-3 sentences (customers, handoffs, crosscheck, master data, settings). They explain rules the page already shows. Fix: one sentence, 15 words at most, and move rules to the Guide page or a field hint.
11. **R-36, negation tails.** "tidak pernah mengesahkan pembelian dengan sendirinya", "tidak pernah menghapus atau memulihkan", "Tidak ada yang ditulis sebelum Anda mengimpor", repeated on several pages. Fix: state the positive behavior once ("Importing adds new projects only").
12. **R-04, one icon for unlike pages.** SLA SOA, SLA Purchasing, Master Data and System share the "system" icon; Technical Reports and Financial Crosscheck share "reports"; Users and PICs share "users". Navigation icons then stop telling pages apart. Fix: distinct relevant icons, or no icon on the administration group.
13. **R-04, Lucide look (note, low effort to keep).** All 20 icon files use Lucide. The icons are relevant, so this is acceptable with a written reason (R-31). Fix: record the reason in `DESIGN.md`; no code change unless the user wants a different set.
14. **R-01, login background gradient.** `login/page.tsx:17` and `password-change/page.tsx:18` use `bg-gradient-to-b from-accent/60 to-background`. It is a faint single-brand tint, so it is probably fine, but no reason is written. Fix: keep with a written reason, or use the flat background.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

15. **R-31 / R-37, no written design direction.** There is no `DESIGN.md`. The palette comment in `globals.css` explains the colors only. Fix: add `DESIGN.md` with one-line reasons for color, type (Geist, chosen as the Next.js default, so a reason or a change is needed, R-06), radius, shadow, icons, and the three dials.
16. **Duplicate heading text on the login page.** Two different cards are both titled "Switch account" (`login/page.tsx:60` and `:101`). Fix: "Continue as…" for remembered accounts, "Recent accounts" for the other.
17. **Ambiguous button labels.** "Forget" and "Remove" next to an account (`login/page.tsx:86,125`) do not say what is forgotten. The `aria-label` does. Fix: visible label "Forget this account" / "Remove from list".
18. **Inconsistent casing in navigation and titles.** "Sales Targets", "Master Data", "Technical Reports", "Audit Logs", "Users and Roles" (Title Case) beside "New project", "All requests", "Notifications" (sentence case). Fix: sentence case everywhere.
19. **Tagline wording.** "Project Request & Fulfillment Tracker" is repeated under the logo on the login page and in the sidebar in uppercase. "Fulfillment" is vague for what the app does (tracks BOQ, RFQ and PO requests between Sales, SOA and Purchasing). Fix: a concrete tagline once the language decision (3) is made.
20. **11px badge text.** `notifications/page.tsx:53` uses `text-[11px] uppercase` white on red for "Revision". Verify contrast and size; raise to 12px sentence case.
